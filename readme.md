**Control**

Desktop
-------
A D, J L, ← → - Move Left & Right
W, I, ↑, Space - Jump
Enter, Shift - Throw Flask

Mobile
------
On the left hand side you press the buttons to move left and right
On the right hand side you press the buttons to jump and to throw flasks

**How to Play**

This little game is a sidescroller. You play Pepe Peligroso and fight against chickens whilst collecting coins and flasks full of tabasko. To beat this game you have to defeat the giant chicken at the end of the small canyon via throwing the collected flasks at it.
